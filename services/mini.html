Description

A miniature commission consists in a set of icons depicting one or more characters. They can be used as profile pictures, emotes, or even tokens for your favorite virtual tabletop!

Such a commission is rather freeform depending on your exact needs, but will typically include portraits or chibis. They may also consist in a simple 2-to-4 frame looping animation. For you TTRPG enthusiasts, you may also request for a custom token frame - the first one is free!

Pricing

All prices are given in USD.

Base prices: $15 for portraits, $25 for chibis
Custom token frame: $5 (the first one is free); only for non-animated portraits Price is doubled for animated miniatures.
